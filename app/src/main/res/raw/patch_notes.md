## Command Panel v0.6.0

Blur for any look, and more control over where your Replay Buffer clips go.

### New
- **Aurora Blur look.** Aurora Glass that blurs whatever is behind the overlay on the bottom screen, like your wallpaper.
- **Blur behind in Theme Studio.** Turn it on for any look under Effects, then set the blur strength and tint.
- **Choose where clips are saved.** Replay Buffer saves to TCP Clips by default, or pick any folder on internal storage.
- **Clips sorted by screen.** Each clip goes into a Top, Bottom or Both folder.
- **Open folder.** Jump straight to your clips in your file manager.

### Changes
- **New default clips folder.** Clips now go to TCP Clips instead of game_clips. Clips you already saved stay in game_clips.
- **Double-press shows what it's saving,** for example "BOTH | Saving 30 sec clip…", even when the overlay is closed.
- **Smaller option samples** in Theme Studio, and the Jelly nav bar sample no longer covers its label.

### Fixes
- **A deleted clips folder falls back to TCP Clips** instead of being recreated.
- **Clip folders with an apostrophe in the name** now save correctly.

See the [v0.5.1 notes](https://github.com/BrianJr03/command-panel/releases/tag/v0.5.1) for the previous release.
