## Command Panel v0.3.0

A guided first run, a new default look, and a home for all your themes.

### New
- **Overlay onboarding.** On first launch the overlay opens on the bottom screen and walks you through picking a look, long-pressing to rearrange, dragging cards, and Quick Launch.
- **Try looks right away.** The first onboarding step lets you tap Aurora Glass, Tactical HUD or Soft Console and see the panel restyle live. The companion follows along and highlights each one in its Built-in list.
- **Hands-on steps.** Long-press and drag steps move on by themselves once you do the gesture, and the panel stays fully usable while you try it.
- **Gamepad controls for both guides.** A goes to the next step, B goes back (or exits on the first step), and Y skips. Each button shows its letter so you know which is which.
- **D-pad and stick pick a look.** On the look step, the D-pad and analog stick move between the three presets.
- **Themes library.** With more than three saved looks, the Themes section shows them as one card with the first three fanned like a deck. Open it for a full screen with Local and Packs tabs.
- **Switch tabs with L1 and R1.** In the Themes library, L1 and R1 flip between Local and Packs, and you can also tap a tab or swipe.
- **Take the tour replays both guides.** It now runs the overlay onboarding first, then the companion tour.

### Changes
- **Aurora Glass is the default look** on a fresh install, instead of the stock look.
- **The companion tour starts after the overlay onboarding**, so only one guide asks for attention at a time.
- **B in the companion tour now goes back** a step instead of skipping. Y skips.
- **Theme Studio returns you where you were.** Closing it lands on the same section and scroll position.

See the [v0.2.1 notes](https://github.com/BrianJr03/command-panel/releases/tag/v0.2.1) for the previous release.
