## Command Panel v0.9.0

Volume keys reach the bottom screen, Quick Launch can bring the panel back, a Joystick Lights tile, and a batch of fixes.

### New
- **Volume keys reach the bottom screen.** Past max or min music volume, the volume keys now step the bottom-screen volume, like the stock assistant.
- **After Quick Launch.** Choose what the panel does after launching an app: close, stay open, or come back when you leave the app.
- **Joystick Lights tile.** Turn the stick lights on or off from the panel. Add it in Tiles.
- **Bigger tiles in big cards.** Quick tile icons grow to fill a larger Quick Tiles card.
- **Experimental section.** A new settings section for features still being tested. The first one is In-game FPS, which counts the frames the game itself draws.

### Fixes
- **Brightness stays put.** Opening the app after opening the lid no longer jumps the top screen to full brightness.
- **Power ring matches the number.** The ring now fills with the watts shown, and the gauge says CHG while charging.
- **Smooth bottom volume slider.** Dragging it is smooth and no longer snaps back.
- **Top volume slider stays in sync.** It follows volume changes while the panel is open.
- **Sound in early clips.** A clip saved right after turning on the Replay Buffer now has sound, and the mic stays in sync with game audio over long sessions.
- **No-sound note.** The Replay Buffer section tells you when clips will have no sound.
- **Steadier FPS counter.** The reading is rounded instead of rounded up.

See the [v0.8.1 notes](https://github.com/BrianJr03/command-panel/releases/tag/v0.8.1) for the previous release.

> **About `tcp-sc-0.9.0.apk`:** an optional helper app, TCP SC. Apps like Back Button Hero can launch it to save a Replay Buffer clip. You only need it for that, and Command Panel can install it for you from its Replay Buffer section. If you install it by hand, install it after Command Panel.
